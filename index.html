Part 2: the findings report

Every answer becomes a dollar figure.

times a week×minutes each×your pay rate=dollars a year

Count it, don't guess it. We use your own pay rates.

We ask "about how many times a week, about how many minutes each", then multiply by 52 weeks and your pay rate. Counting events works better than asking people to estimate.

Illustrative only, not client data: an estimator writing 10 quotes a week at about 60 minutes each is 10 hours a week, about 520 hours a year. At A$53.71 an hour that is about A$27,929 a year.

If you'd rather not share pay rates, we use the Victorian average.

Straight talk: recovered time is capacity, not cash, until it's put to use.
